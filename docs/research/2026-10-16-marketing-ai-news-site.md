# Marketing an AI news site as a solo dev (deep-research, 2026-10-16)

Raw workflow output (incl. refuted claims): `2026-10-16-marketing-ai-news-site.raw.txt`

## Verified findings (all medium/low confidence; sources are secondary)

1. **Distribution over product.** Build promotion into the product (e.g. no-login access). Quality alone brought <5 users. Anecdotal.
   - https://devtools.shingoirie.com/blog/en/indie-dev-app-promotion-first-100-users/
   - https://xor01.substack.com/p/building-isnt-enough-a-developers
2. **The Neuron growth model.** First 100k subscribers from the co-founder's organic LinkedIn; ad revenue then reinvested in Meta paid growth. 100k in 6 months, 500k+ in 24. Self-reported (beehiiv promo case); may not replicate without an existing audience.
   - https://www.beehiiv.com/case-studies/the-neuron.md
3. **There's An AI For That.** Site with traffic first, newsletter launched April 2023 as a retention channel.
   - https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/case-study-there-s-an-ai-for-that.md
4. **Paid subscriptions need a big free base.** Median free-to-paid conversion on beehiiv 0.62% at $10/mo median (~6 payers per 1000 subscribers).
   - https://voxbooster.com/blog/newsletter-statistics-2026
5. **Crowded, risky market.** Digg relaunching as an AI news aggregator (May 2026); Smashing shut down April 2025 despite ~$3.4-4M funding. Bare aggregation/AI summarising is commoditised; need a niche and a point of view.
   - https://feedbagel.com/post/digg-tries-again-this-time-as-an-ai-news-aggregator-techcrunch
   - https://ideaproof.io/failure/smashing
6. **Claude Code skills (third-party, effectiveness untested).** `content-strategy` (Pulizzi: niche, mission, 3-5 content pillars) and `content-refresher` (SEO/GEO refresh of old content).
   - https://claudemarketplaces.com/skills/guia-matthieu/clawfu-skills/content-strategy
   - https://claudemarketplaces.com/skills/aaron-he-zhu/seo-geo-claude-skills/content-refresher
7. **Marketing as a repo/build system** (low confidence, one article): research, positioning, assets, distribution, measurement, kept in git.
   - https://articles.firstaimovers.com/articles/vibe-marketing-developers-ctos-repo-funnel/
