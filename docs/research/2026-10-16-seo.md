# SEO for a curation-only site (research, 2026-10-16)

Three parallel web-research passes: Google's treatment of aggregators, Next.js 16 technical SEO, measurement
tools. Primary sources preferred; the date after each source is its "last updated" date or the date it was fetched.
**[I]** marks inference, not a documented fact. The plan built on this is `plans/seo.md`.

## 1. How Google treats aggregators

- **Spam policies** (developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies, 2026-08-28):
  - *Scaled content abuse* (since March 2024): "Scraping feeds, search results, or other content to generate many
    pages", "Stitching or combining content from different web pages without adding value". Applies however the
    pages are made.
  - *Scraping*: "Reproducing content feeds from other sites without providing some type of unique benefit to the
    user"; "sites dedicated to embedding or compiling content … without substantial added value". This is the
    clause closest to a plain RSS link list.
  - *Doorway abuse*: "substantially similar pages that are closer to search results than a clearly defined,
    browseable hierarchy". This applies to indexable filter combinations.
  - *Site reputation abuse* does not apply (ATTN hosts no third-party content).
- **Helpful content** is part of core ranking since March 2024. Self-assessment
  (developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content, 2026-10-05): "Are you mainly
  summarizing what others have to say without adding much value?"; there is no preferred word count; "How was
  this created?" disclosures for automation are encouraged.
- **Gen-AI content** (…/using-gen-ai-content, 2026-10-01): AI use is fine, but generating many pages without
  added value may violate scaled-content abuse. That covers LLM summaries of linked articles.
- **Mueller on programmatic pages** (Bluesky, 2026-09-07, via seroundtable.com/google-lose-faith-42032.html):
  low-value programmatic pages make "our systems … [lose] faith in your site", and recovery takes time. On a large
  aggregator (2026-10-06, seroundtable.com/google-seo-large-product-aggregator-42232.html): "Convincing search
  engines that this is worthwhile … will be hard."
- Google publishes no safe page count and no minimum content size.

**[I] What this means for ATTN:**
- A page that is only titles and outbound links from RSS sits right on the "reproducing content feeds" line.
- Per-article pages on ATTN's domain would be thin and would lose to the original article anyway.
- The defensible added value is the skill taxonomy, the difficulty levels and types, ordering as a learning path,
  dedup across sources, and short owner-written context per hub.

## 2. What ranks for comparable sites

Semrush estimates, September 2026 data, fetched 2026-10-16:

- **tldr.tech**: ~99.5K organic of ~701K total visits, mostly branded queries.
- **news.ycombinator.com**: branded, plus item pages that carry user comments (original content).
- **roadmap.sh**: ~359K organic; ranks non-brand ("full stack developer" #1) with topic hubs that have original
  structure; ~21K ChatGPT referrals over 6 months.
- **theresanaiforthat.com**: per-item tool profile pages rank for the tool names.
- **futurepedia.io**: organic is now a small share of its traffic.
- **daily.dev** (structure, fetched 2026-10-16):
  - post pages say "daily.dev links to this article rather than hosting it" and carry upvotes and comments;
  - tag pages have a description, top sources and top posts;
  - the sitemap index covers posts, tags, sources and monthly archives.
- **Techmeme**: robots.txt disallows `/search/`, `/r/` and `/goto/`.
- No credible public case study with numbers exists for a link-curation site.

**[I]** Curation sites live on brand, direct visits and distribution. Non-brand search comes from hubs or item pages
that have their own substance.

## 3. Technical (Next.js 16.4, App Router)

- **Metadata** (nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/functions/generate-metadata, 2026-08-19):
  - `metadataBase` is required for relative canonical and OG URLs.
  - `title.template` goes in the root layout.
  - Merging across segments is shallow: a page's `robots` or `openGraph` replaces the parent's whole object.
- **Streaming metadata** (since 15.2):
  - On dynamic pages, metadata may be appended to `<body>` for JS-executing bots. Plain `Googlebot` is not in the
    default `htmlLimitedBots` list (checked in the Next.js `html-bots.ts`, canary, 2026-10-16).
  - Google accepts a robots meta anywhere (…/robots-meta-tag, 2026-03-24).
  - `rel=canonical` must be in `<head>` (…/consolidate-duplicate-urls, 2026-07-10).
  - **Verify with URL Inspection**: every ATTN page is dynamic because `Nav` reads cookies.
- **Faceted navigation** (…/crawling-managing-faceted-navigation, 2025-12-18):
  - If facets need not be in Search, disallow them in robots.txt and allow one unfiltered listing.
  - Canonical and nofollow are "less effective in the long term".
  - Return 404 for a filter combination with no results.
- **Pagination** (…/pagination-and-incremental-page-loading, 2025-12-10): Google ignores rel=next/prev. Filter
  and sort views should be noindex or blocked.
- **Structured data**:
  - The carousel/ItemList rich result needs list items on the same domain (…/carousel, 2026-09-08), so ATTN is
    not eligible.
  - CollectionPage and ItemList are not rich results (search gallery, 2026-06-15).
  - Useful: BreadcrumbList on hubs, and WebSite + Organization on the home page (site name and logo; logo
    ≥112×112).
  - Sitelinks search box was retired in November 2024.
- **Outbound links** (…/qualify-outbound-links, 2025-12-10): editorial links need no rel. Never route article
  clicks through a redirect under a disallowed `/api/` path.
- **Redirects** (…/301-redirects, 2026-04-14): a 307 is not a canonical signal. robots.txt is not a way to keep
  a page out of the index; noindex is (…/block-indexing, 2025-12-10).
- **IndexNow** (indexnow.org/documentation; searchengines.json fetched 2026-10-16):
  - Participants: Bing, Yandex, Seznam, Naver, Yep, Internet Archive, Amazonbot. Not Google.
  - How it works: a key file plus a POST of up to 10,000 URLs.
  - Do not resubmit unchanged URLs.
- **Sitemaps**: Google uses `lastmod` only when it is accurate and ignores `priority` and `changefreq`
  (…/build-sitemap, 2026-07-08). The ping endpoint is gone (2023-06).
- **Core Web Vitals** (…/core-web-vitals, 2025-12-10): LCP ≤2.5 s, INP <200 ms, CLS <0.1.
- **OG images** (`next/og` `ImageResponse`): only ttf, otf or woff fonts, so the repo's woff2 fontsource files
  will not work.

## 4. AI search (GEO)

- **Google AI Overviews and AI Mode** (…/appearance/ai-features, 2025-12-10; …/ai-optimization-guide,
  2026-07-10):
  - "no additional requirements", no special files or markup.
  - The page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet.
  - Google ignores llms.txt (Illyes, July 2025; Mueller compared it to the keywords meta tag).
- **ChatGPT search** needs `OAI-SearchBot` allowed; `GPTBot` (training) is separate
  (developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots, fetched 2026-10-16). Its reliance on Bing's index is reported
  second-hand, not documented by OpenAI.
- **Copilot** is "powered by Bing's search index" (Microsoft Ads blog, 2025-10-08). **DuckDuckGo** sources mostly
  from Bing.
- **Perplexity**: PerplexityBot surfaces sites in its results; allow it.
- **Clicks**: when an AI summary shows, users click less (Pew, 2025-07-22). Citations are visibility more than
  traffic.
- **[Fact, low confidence]** Vendor studies (2026) find "best X" lists among the most-cited page types. This is
  plausible for "how to learn <skill>" hubs.

## 5. Measurement

- **Google Search Console**:
  - Use a **Domain property**, verified by a DNS TXT record (keep the record forever)
    (support.google.com/webmasters/answer/34592, 9008080).
  - Performance report: clicks, impressions, CTR, average position by query, page, country and device. Data lags
    2–3 days; a 24-hour hourly view exists (2024-12). Retention is 16 months.
  - Page indexing statuses to watch: "Crawled – currently not indexed", "Discovered – currently not indexed",
    "Duplicate without user-selected canonical", "URL marked 'noindex'".
  - **Generative AI performance report**, for all sites since 2026-08-31 (developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/
    gen-ai-performance-reports):
    - impressions only;
    - AI Overviews and AI Mode are not split;
    - it is already inside the Web totals, so do not add it to them.
  - Also: custom annotations (2025-11), a branded-query filter (needs volume), and Insights built in (2025-06).
- **Bing Webmaster Tools**:
  - Import the site from GSC in one step.
  - **AI Performance** report (public preview since 2026-02): Copilot and Bing AI citations, grounding queries,
    cited pages. No clicks.
- **PostHog**:
  - A built-in **AI channel type** (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, Copilot;
    posthog.com/docs/data/channel-type). Organic Search is matched before AI.
  - ChatGPT appends `utm_source=chatgpt.com`.
  - A **Google Search Console data-warehouse source** exists (posthog.com/docs/cdp/sources/google-search-console);
    1M synced rows/month are free.
  - Cookieless (declined) visitors get no cross-visit first-touch. Use the session-level channel type.
- **Free extras**:
  - Ahrefs Webmaster Tools: site audit and backlinks for verified sites.
  - Screaming Frog free: up to 500 URLs.
  - PageSpeed Insights: field data will be "insufficient" at first.
  - Rich Results Test.
  - Paid trackers (Semrush, Profound, Otterly) are overkill pre-traction.
- **Domain**:
  - The TLD gives no ranking advantage; .ai and .io are treated as generic
    (…/managing-multi-regional-sites, 2025-12-10).
  - No exact-match-domain bonus (Mueller).
  - Server location is not a signal.
  - Choose on memorability and renewal price.
- **Timelines** (…/ask-google-to-recrawl, 2025-12-10; SEO starter guide):
  - Crawling takes days to weeks.
  - Changes take hours to months.
  - No confirmed sandbox.
  - New pages are found mostly through links.
  - **[I]** Expect hubs indexed in 1–2 weeks, first non-brand clicks in 1–3 months, and meaningful non-brand
    traffic in 6–12+ months, if ever.
